WillowVale Clock privacy

WillowVale Clock is used by WillowVale Clinic staff to record the times they start and finish work, for wages.

What is recorded

  • Your name and the email address of the Google account you sign in with.
  • The time you clock in or out.
  • Your phone's location at the moment you tap Clock in or Clock out, and how far that is from the clinic. Location is not tracked at any other time.

Where it is kept

Records are stored in a Google Sheet owned and managed by WillowVale Clinic. Only clinic management can see it. The records are kept as employee time and wage records.

Google sign-in

Signing in with Google only shares your name and email address with the clinic. The app does not get access to your Gmail, contacts or files.
